Black Ice

About

Black Ice (also known as Lovelogic) is an experimental singer/songwriter whose music has many late night thoughts, nostalgic moments and painful memories behind them.

Having classical training mixed with a modern upbringing makes his sound uniquely his own but able to relate to anyone who listens. As a member of Creek Bay Records, Black Ice is making memories while carving his name into the world, one track at a time
